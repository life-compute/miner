Get started in 3 commands

Works on Linux, macOS, and Windows. Requires an NVIDIA GPU (8 GB+ VRAM) and Docker.

1

Download & install

Pulls the Docker image and checks your GPU drivers, Python, and CUDA version.

$ curl -sSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/life-compute/miner/main/install.sh | bash

Windows: irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/life-compute/miner/main/install.ps1 | iex

2

Connect your Solana wallet

Generates a new keypair or imports an existing address. Your $LIFE earnings go here.

$ ~/.life-compute/bin/life-compute wallet connect
3

Start mining

Launches the miner with GPU passthrough. Then open http://localhost:8765 in your browser to see the dashboard.

$ docker run -d --gpus all --name life-compute-miner \
  -v ~/.life-compute:/root/.life-compute \
  -p 8765:8765 \
  ghcr.io/life-compute/miner:latest

How it works

Four steps from your GPU to a verified on-chain result.

🧬

Target assigned

The network assigns your miner a cancer protein target (e.g. KRAS G12C, TP53) from the on-chain registry.

⚡

GPU scores molecules

Your GPU runs Boltz2 structure prediction, computing binding affinity (ΔG kcal/mol) for candidate drug molecules.

⛓

Result submitted on-chain

Your best hit is submitted to the LIFE Compute Solana program. Three validators independently rescore it.

💰

$LIFE minted

Confirmed results trigger the mint_reward instruction. $LIFE tokens land in your wallet automatically.
